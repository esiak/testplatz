Einbürgerungstest appointment search

One page to find citizenship test dates across Bavarian test centres, by distance, instead of checking 5–10 sites by hand. The central claim, that the dates can be read by machine, held up for about 70% of VHS sites.

Locked decisions

  • Search only. No booking; every result links to the provider's page.
  • v1 = Bavaria only: all ~116 BAMF test centres (≈101 VHS + ≈15 others). Tests need residence in Bavaria.
  • Per date: date/time, place, free / booked out / unknown, deadline, how to register, link.
  • Coverage A: automatic readers for KuferWeb (~30 sites) and the ITEM KG TYPO3 setup (~25 sites, incl. MVHS). Every other centre is shown as "not checked automatically, see website". A gap never looks like "nothing available".
  • Straight-line km distance, with a link out to directions.
  • Check 2–4×/day, identified bot, robots.txt respected, drop any centre on request. "Last checked" is shown everywhere.
  • History from day one: a first-seen time per date gives a "new since yesterday" badge.
  • Free public side project, near-zero running cost, no personal data stored.

Version 2

Alerts: "a new date or free seat appeared within X km of me." The real pain is not knowing when something new appears; v1's history makes this cheap to add.

Rejected, and why

  • Booking through the site: out of scope; the provider's own process stays the source of truth.
  • Region-first 100% coverage: partial coverage with visible gaps helps more people sooner.
  • "Registration opens at…" as a field: the Bavarian sample sites don't publish it. They publish deadlines (3–5 weeks before) and booked-out flags.
  • Nationwide from day one: formats differ per state (Berlin portal, Pankow email forms, NRW PDFs).

Weak points that survived

  • ~23 VHS sites of unknown or bot-blocking build, plus ~15 non-VHS providers: links only.
  • Readers break silently when Kufer or ITEM KG change their markup. "Last checked" makes this visible, but someone has to fix it.
  • The ~70% vendor share comes from a homepage fingerprint, not from checking each site's test page.
  • "No competitor exists" rests on two web searches.
  • Some dates can't be booked remotely (Bad Kissingen: in person, cash only).
