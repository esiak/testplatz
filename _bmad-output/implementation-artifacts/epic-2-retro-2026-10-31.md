### Coverage and data (what the user actually gets)

**F1. Five ITEM KG centres have never produced an outcome.** [live] Würzburg, Erding, Cham, Straubing and Kelheim have no entry in `data/state/state.json` at all, yet each host has 4 visits in `data/state/visits.json` (the 10/29 14:03, 10/29 17:58, 10/30 09:30 and 10:11 runs). The snapshot's ITEM KG system line reads `centres_covered: 21, centres_ok: 15, centres_failed: 1`, so five are neither. Story 2.4 names Würzburg and Erding in its user story, and both have committed fixtures that pass. From a home machine on 2026-10-31 each list page answered `200` with no redirect, and the project's own `parseRobots` allows each URL (Erding and Straubing serve robots.txt as 404 = allow all). Merge stores nothing for `skipped` (`pipeline/stages/merge.ts:70`), so the committed data cannot say why; the cause is in the CI run log lines (`run.ts:159`), which were not reachable. *Disposition:* fix now (diagnose from CI logs). *Prevention:* skipped outcomes leave no trace anywhere a later reader can see; a run summary in state or the snapshot would have shown this on day one.

**F2. MVHS (München), the largest centre, fails on every run.** [live][spec-deferred 2.4] `last_result: failed`, `last_success_at: null`. `data/directory/itemkg-note.md` explains: MVHS publishes no start time, `RawListing` requires one, so the reader fails with `date-unparseable`. MVHS also has no coordinates (`data/directory/geocode-note.md`, Epic 1 action item 2 still open). Live check: "München", 10 km, gives "0 dates and 9 unchecked centres". *Disposition:* fix now; needs a contract change (a date with unknown time) → spec reconciliation. *Prevention:* the 2.4 decision to fail rather than guess was right, but it was recorded only as a deferral while the story's own user story promised MVHS.

**F3. "In person" registration suppresses the status even when other methods exist.** [live] 81 of 100 published dates have status `unknown`; 73 of those have `registration_method: in_person` (56 ITEM KG, 17 KuferWeb). ITEM KG's `flagSignals` returns no signals whenever `method === 'in_person'` (`pipeline/readers/itemkg/index.ts:222`), and `registrationMethodOf` returns `in_person` on the first sentence that matches "Anmeldung erfolgt persönlich" (`:186-197`). Story 2.4 says only *in-person-only* registration gives `unknown`. Checked live on 2026-10-31: the VHS Fichtelgebirge Marktredwitz page says registration is in person, *and* "Alternativ ist auch eine Anmeldung per E-Mail möglich" — published as `in_person` / `unknown`. KuferWeb has its own, different in-person regex (`pipeline/readers/kuferweb/index.ts:406`) and does not suppress status on it. *Disposition:* fix now (spec deviation, FR10). *Prevention:* the two readers each wrote their own registration rules (see F15 duplication).

**F4. Coverage falls short of the epic goal.** [live] 34 centres are `covered` (ITEM KG 21, KuferWeb 13) against "about 55". Of those, 26 read `ok`, 3 `failed` (MVHS, Landshut, Eching) and 5 never ran (F1). 100 dates are published. *Disposition:* spec reconciliation — either restate the epic goal as 34 confirmed centres or carry the gap forward explicitly. *Prevention:* Stories 2.3/2.4 recorded "the list of centres confirmed" but nothing compared it with the epic's number.

**F5. Story 2.11's Herrsching example now works.** [live] The 2.11 deferral (Herrsching dates still `location: centre`) is resolved by the 10/30 pipeline runs: the snapshot has 73 `venue` / 27 `centre` dates, and "Herrsching", 10 km shows both VHS Starnberger See dates at Jahnstraße 10 at 1 km. All 27 `centre` dates are at their centre's own address. *Disposition:* close the 2.11 deferral.

### Cross-story defects (no single session saw both sides)

**F6. Every push to `main` spends one of each site's 4 daily visits.** [live][spec-deferred 2.6][review: verification-gap] `pipeline.yml:7-8` runs the full collection on `push: main`. After the four runs on 10/29–10/30, every host in `visits.json` sits at 4/4; the 11:23 run (operator push `348e6f1`) could only reach Eching, the one new host. The publish job also checks out `${{ github.sha }}` (`pipeline.yml:34`), so a run queued behind another reads a visit log that is missing the previous run's visits and can send a 5th visit within 24 h (NFR3). The About text says sites are read "once a day". *Disposition:* fix now. *Prevention:* 2.2 (budget) and 2.6 (triggers) were built in separate sessions; neither tested the workflow-level interaction.

**F7. Same-minute ids shift, so history attaches to the wrong date.** [review: adversarial + edge-case][spec-deferred 2.5] Ids `#2`, `#3` are positional within one run (`pipeline/stages/normalise.ts:131-136`); merge keys `first_seen_at`, `baseline` and tombstones by id (`merge.ts:42-51`). When a same-minute sibling appears or disappears, a surviving date inherits another's history. *Disposition:* defer, but it is a **prerequisite for Story 3.1** ("New" badge reads `first_seen_at`/`baseline`).

**F8. The two readers disagree on what the detail-page cap means.** [review: adversarial + edge-case] With more than 30 test links, KuferWeb parses the 30 fetched pages and returns `ok` (`kuferweb/index.ts:469-507`), so merge tombstones the rest; ITEM KG fails the whole centre with `detail-page` (`itemkg/index.ts:337-345`). The cap is defined once (`shared/reader.ts:64-67`). The largest centre today has 14 dates, so this is latent. *Disposition:* defer; define the cap semantics in the reader contract.

**F9. The rejected-push replay can roll back newer data.** [review: adversarial + edge-case] The `--from-collect` replay applies an older `runAt` over newer upstream state with no recency check (`.github/scripts/commit-public.sh:95-97`, `merge.ts:63-81`); the data-only rebase path accepts a textual merge of `state.json`/`visits.json` without validating it (`commit-public.sh:87-91`). *Disposition:* defer (needs two overlapping runs, which F6 makes more likely).

**F10. A failed run after merge loses that run's visits.** [review: adversarial][spec-deferred 2.6] Merge writes `visits.json`, but if publish or a later step fails, the job ends before `commit-public.sh` and the visits never reach the repo (`run.ts:171-190`, `pipeline.yml:43-62`). *Disposition:* defer, fold into the F6 fix.

**F11. Distance can be measured to the centre while the card shows the test address.** [review: adversarial + edge-case] When locate falls back (unparseable, miss, cap), the listing keeps its own `address` but gets the centre's coordinates (`locate.ts:46,181-182`); the card never reads `listing.location` (`web/src/search/result-card.tsx:78,123-127`). About says distance is measured to the test address. In today's snapshot all 27 fallback dates are at the centre's own address, so no card is wrong yet. *Disposition:* defer.

**F12. The "no map position" notice counts all of Bavaria.** [live][review: adversarial + edge-case][spec-2-11 triage] `hiddenUnlocated` counts every centre with null coordinates, not limited by radius, including ones whose dates are already shown (`web/src/search/search.ts:459`). "Herrsching", 10 km reads "8 centres have no map position yet and may be nearby". *Disposition:* defer, low.

**F14. The reader contract (2.1) fixed the output shape but not these shared interpretations. *Prevention:* move shared interpretation helpers into `shared/` before a third reader.

### Other review findings, deferred or accepted

- **Accepted:** the claim "an unchanged run produces no data commit" (`publish.ts:5-7`) can never hold, because `last_checked_at` changes on every run (`merge.ts:53,74`). Daily data commits are fine; fix the comment.
- **Deferred (low):** the count line counts unknown-distance dates as "within R km" (`search-page.tsx:63-66`); Nominatim hits with no postcode skip the postcode check and are cached for good (`pipeline/fetch/nominatim.ts:100`); no back-off after Nominatim 429/5xx (`locate.ts:139-157`); a detail page blocked by budget or robots fails the centre instead of skipping it (`pipeline/fetch/centre.ts:53-60`); one 00:00 placeholder row fails a whole ITEM KG centre (`itemkg/index.ts:96,361`); failed centres keep their old status chip without a "latest check failed" hint (`result-card.tsx:162`).
- **Dropped:** "a centre in range whose dates are all outside the radius disappears" (`search.ts:376-377`) is the behaviour Story 2.11 asks for. Three robustness nits (`maxLookups` NaN, historic-year offsets, `venue` without coordinates passing the schema) have no realistic trigger.
